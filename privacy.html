Privacy policy

Talk Health · last updated 27 September 2026

Who uses it

Talk Health is a personal tool with a single user: the person who built it, using it on their own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

With the user's consent, it reads activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep data from the Google Health API. Access is read-only. It never writes to, changes or deletes anything in the user's Google account.

Where the data goes

Daily summaries are saved to private notes on the user's own computer and devices. The data is not sent to any server run by this app. It is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page under Security, Third-party apps and services. Deleting the local notes deletes the saved data.
